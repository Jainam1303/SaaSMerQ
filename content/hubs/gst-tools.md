## Why GST deserves its own tool collection

GST in India is not one rate. Supplies fall into 5%, 12%, 18% and 28% slabs (with exemptions and special cases). B2B invoices need HSN or SAC codes, taxable value, and explicit tax columns. Consumers see inclusive prices on packaging and need reverse calculation to find pre-tax value.

Generic percentage calculators miss CGST/SGST symmetry and IGST routing. ## Adding and removing GST correctly

Forward calculation: **Tax = Base × (Rate ÷ 100)** and **Gross = Base + Tax**. Reverse calculation when MRP includes tax: **Base = Gross ÷ (1 + Rate ÷ 100)**. Rounding per invoice rules can create minor differences versus ERP systems — round line-wise as your accountant recommends.

The GST Calculator updates instantly as you toggle slabs or switch between add and remove modes. Use it when validating supplier bills, building e-commerce price lists, or teaching staff why 18% on ₹1,000 is not ₹180 when the price already includes tax.

## CGST, SGST and IGST on invoices

Same-state sales divide GST equally between CGST and SGST — each half the headline rate. Inter-state sales charge IGST at the full rate to the central government, avoiding double state taxation. Wrong selection on invoices causes ITC mismatches and reconciliation pain during GSTR filing.

The GST Invoice Generator selects intra-state or inter-state supply and computes tax columns per line. Enter supplier and buyer GSTIN when registered, add HSN codes for goods, and print or save PDF via the browser. For bills without tax breakdown, the [Invoice Generator](/tools/invoice-generator) offers a cleaner proforma format.

Businesses selling nationwide should default IGST for distant clients and CGST/SGST for local retail — the generator makes the split visible before you send PDFs to customers.

MerQPrime tools educate and format — they do not file GSTR-1 or validate GSTIN against the government portal in real time. You are responsible for registration thresholds, e-invoice mandates for large taxpayers, and e-way bill rules for goods movement.

## Audit trails and reconciliation

Match calculator outputs to GSTR-2B and purchase registers monthly. Small rounding differences between line-wise and invoice-total GST are normal; document your rounding policy for auditors. When vendors quote inclusive prices, use remove-GST mode before comparing bids from suppliers in different states.

E-commerce operators selling across India should default IGST in the invoice generator for distant warehouses while using CGST/SGST for local fulfilment centres. Training staff on which dropdown to select prevents credit mismatches during annual reconciliation.

Keep a printed cheat sheet of current slab rates near your billing desk — when rates change in notifications, update the cheat sheet and run one test invoice before bulk billing season starts.
