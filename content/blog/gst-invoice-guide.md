A GST tax invoice is mandatory for registered suppliers selling goods or services in India. It must show taxable value, GST rate and tax split correctly.

## Mandatory fields

Typical GST invoices include:

- Supplier name, address and **GSTIN**  
- Buyer name and address (GSTIN for B2B)  
- Invoice number and date  
- **HSN/SAC** codes for goods and services  
- Taxable value, GST rate and tax amount  
- **CGST + SGST** (same state) or **IGST** (inter-state)  

## CGST, SGST and IGST

- **Same-state sale:** tax is split equally into CGST and SGST (e.g. 18% → 9% + 9%).  
- **Inter-state sale:** full rate as IGST (e.g. 18% IGST).
