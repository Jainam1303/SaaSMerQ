## Why create a UPI QR code online?

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) moved India to instant bank-to-bank transfers. QR codes made collection even faster: the customer opens any UPI app, scans, confirms, and pays. For merchants, QR means:

- No card machine rental or MDR on many personal/small merchant flows
- Faster lines at billing counters
- Fewer mistakes from manually typing VPAs
- Easy sharing on WhatsApp for remote payments

Creating a QR **online** beats visiting a bank branch for a standee file — you control payee name, optional amount and note, and can regenerate instantly if you change VPAs.

## What you need before you start

1. **Active UPI ID (VPA)** — From Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or your bank app (format: `name@bank`).
2. **Display name** — The business or person name payers should see.
3. **Decision on amount** — Static (customer enters amount) vs fixed amount per bill.
4. **Optional note** — Invoice number, table ID or membership month for reconciliation.

You do not need a GSTIN or merchant account for basic UPI QR collection, though businesses should follow tax and bookkeeping rules separately.

## Best practices for shop display

- Print minimum 3×3 cm for reliable scanning under shop lighting.
- Use black QR on white background; avoid glossy reflections.
- Place at eye level near the cash counter.
- Add text: “Scan to pay via UPI” in English and local language if helpful.
- Keep a backup QR image if you rebrand or change VPAs.

## Security reminders

- A UPI QR only **receives** money — it cannot withdraw from your account.
- Never share UPI PINs or OTPs with customers or staff.
- Use a business-linked VPA for commercial receipts when possible.
- Regenerate QR if you suspect your VPA was misprinted or shared incorrectly.

## Troubleshooting failed scans

- **Wrong VPA** — Regenerate after correcting the ID.
- **Blurry print** — Reprint at higher resolution.
- **Old QR after bank switch** — Update VPA in app and create new QR.
- **App camera issues** — Ask customer to use in-app “Scan QR” not generic camera if unsupported.

## Frequently asked workflow questions

**Can I use the same QR on multiple shops?** Yes if the same VPA receives for all locations — but reconciliation per branch may need separate VPAs or careful note discipline.

**Does QR work without internet for the payer?** The payer’s UPI app needs connectivity to authorize payment; your printed QR is offline — only the scan triggers an online authorization.

**Can I embed logo in center of UPI QR?** Avoid heavy logo overlays that break scannability; keep QR high contrast and test after any design edits.
