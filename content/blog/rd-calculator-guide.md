A recurring deposit (RD) lets you save a fixed amount every month while earning bank interest. It is ideal for disciplined saving toward a specific goal — a vacation, gadget or wedding fund.

## RD maturity formula

Indian banks typically use:

**M = R × [(1+i)^n − 1] / i × (1+i)**

- **R** = monthly deposit  
- **i** = monthly interest rate (annual ÷ 12)  
- **n** = number of months  

## RD vs SIP

RDs offer predictable bank returns with low risk. SIPs in mutual funds can deliver higher long-term growth but with market volatility. Many savers use RDs for short goals and SIPs for longer horizons.

## Post office and bank RDs

Post office RDs run for 5 years; banks offer flexible tenures. Compare rates and premature withdrawal rules before opening an account.
