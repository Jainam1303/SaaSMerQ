UPI QR codes let customers pay by scanning with any UPI app — PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM and bank apps. For shops, freelancers and events, a printed UPI QR is often the fastest way to collect money.

## What is a UPI QR code?

It is a QR encoding of a **UPI payment URI** (often called a paam string) that includes:

- Payee VPA (Virtual Payment Address) e.g. `name@upi`
- Optional payee name
- Optional amount
- Optional transaction note

When scanned, the payer’s app opens with fields pre-filled.

## Static vs dynamic QR

| Type | Amount | Use case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Static | Customer enters | Shop counter, printed standee |
| Dynamic | Fixed in QR | Specific invoice amount |

MerQPrime’s generator supports both patterns depending on what you enter.

## Setting up your VPA

1. Create a UPI ID in your bank or wallet app.
2. Verify the display name customers will see.
3. Test with a small ₹1 payment before printing large standees.

## Display tips for businesses

- Print at high contrast; laminate for durability.
- Place near the billing counter at eye level.
- Add “Scan to pay via UPI” in English and local language if helpful.
- Keep a backup QR if you change VPAs.

## Security

- Do not share OTPs or PINs — QR only initiates payment; customer authorizes in app.
- Use your official business VPA, not personal accounts, for commercial receipts when possible.
