Unit conversion translates a measurement from one unit to another within the same physical quantity — metres to kilometres, Celsius to Fahrenheit, litres to gallons and more.

## Why conversion matters

- **Travel** — speed limits and distances abroad.
- **Cooking** — volume and weight in recipes.
- **Fitness** — body weight in kg vs lb.
- **Real estate** — square feet vs square metres.
- **Engineering homework** — SI vs imperial systems.

## How conversion works

Most units convert through a **base unit**:

1. Convert source value to base (e.g. metres for length).
2. Convert base to target unit.

**Example:** 5 kilometres → metres = 5 × 1000 = 5000 m → miles = 5000 ÷ 1609.344 ≈ 3.11 mi

## Temperature is special

Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin use **formulas**, not simple factors:

- °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
- K = °C + 273.15

- Watch US vs UK gallons (MerQPrime uses US gallon).
- Do not convert across categories (length ≠ weight).
